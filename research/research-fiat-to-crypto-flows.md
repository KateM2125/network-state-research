## Executive Summary

Between January 2024 and April 2026, the global cryptocurrency market underwent a structural transformation driven by ETF approvals, institutional adoption, regulatory clarity, and stablecoin proliferation. This brief maps the mechanisms, volumes, and motivations behind the movement of fiat currency into digital assets — from retail on-ramps to sovereign wealth fund allocations — providing a data-rich foundation for KOL content targeting an audience with investment banking and fintech literacy.

### 4.4 Inflation Hedging

The digital gold / inflation hedge thesis for Bitcoin has produced **mixed empirical results** in 2024--2025:

- Since BTC ETF launch (Jan 2024), **gold has outperformed Bitcoin by 19%**
- Silver surged +152% YTD vs Bitcoin's -6.18% decline in 2025
- Bitcoin's correlation with S&P 500 has increased, indicating a shift toward behaving as a conventional risk asset
- However, correlation with gold rises during periods of systemic stress, suggesting gradual convergence toward store-of-value behavior

**Institutional framing:**
- BlackRock's thesis: Bitcoin as a "Macro Mirror" reflecting sovereign debt and currency debasement concerns
- Fidelity Digital Assets (Feb 2024 paper): Bitcoin as an "aspirational store of value" — not yet proven but structurally positioned
- WisdomTree models: Bitcoin + gold as complementary hedge portfolio

**Emerging market reality:** In countries with >20% annual inflation (Argentina, Turkey, Nigeria), crypto adoption remains strongly correlated with currency depreciation, regardless of Bitcoin's global USD performance.

*Sources: [ResearchGate BTC Macro Hedge](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/397786784), [CNBC Digital Gold](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/12/05/bitcoin-digital-gold-crypto-store-of-value.html), [Fidelity Digital Assets](https://www.fidelitydigitalassets.com/sites/g/files/djuvja3256/files/acquiadam/1125481.2.0%20-%20FDA%20Bitcoin%20as%20an%20Aspirational%20Store%20of%20Value%20Revisited%20V2.pdf), [WisdomTree](https://www.wisdomtree.com/investments/-/media/us-media-files/documents/resource-library/market-insights/wisdomtree-commentary/bitcoin-and-gold-model-forecasts.pdf)*

## 5. Regulatory Landscape

### 5.1 Australia: Corporations Amendment (Digital Assets Framework) Bill 2025

**Status: PASSED — received Royal Assent (April 1, 2026)**

This is Australia's first dedicated digital asset regulatory framework.

**Key provisions:**
- Creates two new regulated categories under the Corporations Act:
  1. **Digital asset platforms** (hold crypto on behalf of users)
  2. **Tokenized custody platforms** (hold real-world assets, issue corresponding digital tokens)
- Operators must obtain an **Australian Financial Services Licence (AFSL)** from ASIC
- Same core rules as brokers/fund managers: client asset safeguarding, standardised disclosures, dispute resolution, compensation arrangements
- **Scope:** Covers cryptocurrencies (Bitcoin, stablecoins) and tokenised real-world assets (bonds, property, commodities)

**Economic significance:** A$24B annual opportunity from tokenised markets (~1% of GDP), per Digital Finance CRC and industry estimates.

*Sources: [Parliament of Australia](https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Bills_Legislation/bd/bd2526/26bd040), [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/04/01/australia-passes-crypto-licensing-bill-as-ausd24-billion-opportunity-comes-into-focus), [Treasury Ministers](https://ministers.treasury.gov.au/ministers/daniel-mulino-2025/media-releases/new-digital-asset-laws-unlock-innovation-and-safeguard), [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/396009/australia-passes-crypto-bill)*

### 5.2 EU: Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA)

**Status: FULLY APPLICABLE (since 30 Dec 2024)**

**Timeline:**
- Entered into force: 2023
- ART/EMT rules applied: 30 Jun 2024

**Implementation progress:**

- >40 CASP licenses issued across EU member states
- Interim implementation report due 30 Jun 2025

**Stablecoin impact:** CASPs must delist non-MiCA-compliant stablecoins. This has pushed issuers toward EU compliance and influenced global stablecoin market structure.

*Sources: [ESMA MiCA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica), [Skadden MiCA Update](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2025/07/mica-update-six-months-in-application), [Sumsub MiCA 2026](https://sumsub.com/blog/crypto-regulations-in-the-european-union-markets-in-crypto-assets-mica/)*

### 5.3 United States

**Status: RAPIDLY EVOLVING (pro-industry shift)**

**Key developments:**
- **GENIUS Act** (stablecoins): Signed into law Jul 2025; federal framework for payment stablecoins; 18-month implementation period
- **CLARITY Act**: Passed House Jul 2025; defines regulatory perimeter between CFTC and SEC
- **SAB 121 repealed**: Banks can now custody crypto assets
- **Strategic Bitcoin Reserve**: Established at federal level
- **SEC "Project Crypto"**: Chair Paul Atkins launched initiative for "Regulation Crypto" rulemakings in 2026; includes "token taxonomy" and "innovation exemption"
- **CFTC**: Nominee Michael Selig expected to maintain pro-innovation stance

**Structural shift:** The U.S. moved from adversarial regulation (Gensler-era SEC) to collaborative framework-building in under 18 months.

*Sources: [Kroll 2025 Review](https://www.kroll.com/en/publications/financial-compliance-regulation/crypto-comes-age-in-2025), [SEC Press Release](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026-30-sec-clarifies-application-federal-securities-laws-crypto-assets), [The Block Regulation 2026](https://www.theblock.co/post/383241/crypto-regulation-2026-sec-ambitious-agenda-empowered-cftc), [Chainalysis 2025 Roundup](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2025-crypto-regulatory-round-up/)*

### 5.4 Singapore (MAS)

**Status: TIGHTENING AND EXPANDING**

- **Digital Token Service Providers (DTSP) regime:** From 30 Jun 2025, DTSPs serving offshore customers must be licensed
- MAS will issue licenses "only in exceptional cases" — high bar
- **Requirements:** S$250K minimum base capital, Singapore-based compliance officer, annual audits, AML/CFT compliance
- **Licensing progress:** 13 new DPT licenses issued in 2024 (total: 29 licensees)
- **Extraterritorial scope expanded:** Captures firms serving overseas clients from Singapore

*Sources: [MAS Media Release](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2025/mas-clarifies-regulatory-regime-for-digital-token-service-providers), [Reed Smith](https://www.reedsmith.com/en/perspectives/2025/06/mas-finalises-clarifies-regulatory-regime-digital-token-service-providers), [Cointelegraph Singapore](https://cointelegraph.com/explained/break-singapores-new-crypto-rules-and-you-could-face-200k-fine-or-jail)*

### 5.5 Hong Kong

**Status: LICENSING REGIME OPERATIONAL**

- **Stablecoin licensing:** From 1 Aug 2025, all stablecoin issuers in HK must hold HKMA license
- HKMA launched stablecoin issuer sandbox
- Licensed exchanges operational (e.g., HashKey, OSL)
- Positioning as Asia's regulated crypto hub alongside Singapore

*Sources: [Sahm Capital](https://www.sahmcapital.com/news/content/why-singapore-outpaces-hong-kong-in-attracting-crypto-operators-2025-04-08), [TRM Labs Global Policy](https://www.trmlabs.com/reports-and-whitepapers/global-crypto-policy-review-outlook-2024-25-report)*

### 5.6 South Korea

**Status: PHASE 1 LIVE, PHASE 2 IN DEVELOPMENT**

**Virtual Asset User Protection Act (enacted 18 Jul 2023, effective 19 Jul 2024):**
- Customer deposits must be held at banks
- VASPs must segregate customer deposits and crypto
- Must maintain insurance coverage against security incidents
- Regulates price manipulation and unfair trading
- FSC authorised to supervise, inspect, and sanction VASPs

**Phase 2 (in development):**
- **Digital Asset Basic Act**: Comprehensive framework covering issuance, distribution, and regulation of crypto-asset businesses
- Feb 2025: FSC announced plans to gradually allow institutional participation in virtual asset markets (currently retail-only)

**Market context:** South Korea is APAC's second-largest crypto market by value received, with crypto traded "almost like equities" — liquid, speculative, and mainstream. Upbit dominates domestic exchange volume.

*Sources: [FSC Press Release](https://www.fsc.go.kr/eng/pr010101/82683), [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2024-07-18/south-korea-act-to-regulate-cryptocurrency-markets-goes-into-effect/), [Gamma Law](https://gammalaw.com/seoul-searching-is-south-koreas-new-crypto-regulation-the-blueprint-for-global-standards/), [Lightspark Korea Guide](https://www.lightspark.com/knowledge/is-crypto-legal-in-south-korea)*
